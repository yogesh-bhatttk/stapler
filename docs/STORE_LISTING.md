# Chrome Web Store Listing Assets

## 1. Text Assets

### Extension Name
Stapler — Offline PDF Tools

### Short Description (max 132 chars)
Merge, split, compress, sign, and redact PDFs entirely offline in your browser. Zero uploads, zero accounts, zero limits.

### Detailed Description (max 16,000 chars)
**Stapler** is the ultimate offline PDF toolkit designed for privacy, speed, and simplicity. 

Have you ever needed to quickly merge two PDFs or compress a large document, but hesitated to upload your sensitive files to a random online converter? With Stapler, everything happens **locally in your browser**. Your files never leave your device.

**Key Features:**
- **Merge PDFs**: Combine multiple PDF files into one seamlessly. Reorder and rotate pages before merging.
- **Split PDFs**: Extract specific pages or split a large document into individual files.
- **Compress PDFs**: Reduce file size without losing quality, perfect for emailing large documents.
- **Sign & Redact**: Add signatures or blackout sensitive information securely.
- **Offline First**: Works entirely offline after installation. No internet connection required.
- **No Limits**: Process as many files as you want. No file size limits, no daily quotas.

**Privacy Guarantee:**
We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your files. All processing is done using WebAssembly (Wasm) directly on your device. We respect your data privacy above all.

## 2. Image Assets Checklist

To publish on the Chrome Web Store, you will need the following image assets:

- [ ] **Store Icon (128x128):** A clear, recognizable icon representing the extension (png format).
- [ ] **Promotional Marquee (1400x560):** A visually appealing banner to feature your extension (jpg or png).
- [ ] **Screenshots (1280x800 or 640x400):** 1 to 5 screenshots showing the extension's user interface and main features.

*(Note: Ensure your screenshots highlight the "Offline" and "Privacy" aspects of the tool).*
